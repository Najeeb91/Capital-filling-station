Automatic Fuel Flow

Closing − Opening = Meter Movement
Meter Movement − NAT = Net Sale
Net Sale × Retail Price = Revenue
Opening Stock + Purchase − Net Sale = Closing Stock
Important correction: NAT must not be added to stock again after using Net Sale. Adding NAT twice would double-count the tested fuel. The equivalent gross-movement formula is Opening + Purchase − Movement + NAT.
